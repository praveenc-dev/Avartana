Foundation in Nattuvangam

About this Course

This course forms the fundamental base for learning the art of Nattuvangam. Participants will develop strong rhythmic awareness by learning to accurately recite rhythmic syllables (sollukattu) in a steady tempo and practising a series of structured exercises designed to strengthen their sense of rhythm.

Students will be introduced to reading and understanding rhythmic notations, the use of the Thattukazhi, and the essential techniques required for playing it with clarity and precision. The course also covers the basic grammar of jathis, providing participants with a clear understanding of how rhythmic patterns are constructed.

Through guided practice, participants will learn how to coordinate the playing of the Thattukazhi while reciting jathis simultaneously, exploring various rhythmic combinations that build confidence and control. In addition, the course introduces basic choreography techniques, helping dancers and teachers understand how rhythm interacts with movement.

Designed and personally mentored by Dr. Ranjani Ghoshal, one of the world's leading Nattuvanars, this comprehensive Level 1 Certification Program lays a strong and systematic foundation for anyone who wishes to understand, perform, teach, choreograph, or conduct Bharatanatyam with confidence and authority.

By the end of the program, participants will have developed a strong foundation in rhythm, clarity in recitation, and the essential skills required to begin their journey in Nattuvangam.
