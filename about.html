What is Avartana?

Where Rhythm
Becomes Art

Avartana is a dedicated learning space for Bharatanatyam, Nattuvangam, rhythm, and Indian classical dance repertoire. We bring together traditional knowledge and structured learning to help dancers understand not only the movements, but also the rhythm, music, expression, and artistry behind every performance.

Our approach is designed for learners at every stage — from aspiring dancers and dedicated students to teachers and future Nattuvanars. Through focused training, guided practice, and meaningful repertoire, we help every learner build confidence, discipline, and artistic depth.
