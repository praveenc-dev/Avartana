Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

Nurturing the next generation of artists through tradition, rhythm, creativity, and excellence.

Our Vision

Preserving Tradition, Inspiring the Future

To become a globally recognized academy for Bharatanatyam, Nattuvangam, rhythm, and Indian classical dance education — nurturing confident artists who preserve tradition while embracing creativity, innovation, and contemporary learning.

Our Mission

Empowering Artists Through Authentic Learning

Our mission is to make authentic Indian classical dance education accessible to learners across the world while developing strong foundations in rhythm, technique, Nattuvangam, repertoire, and performance.

  • Preserve the richness and values of Indian classical dance.
  • Build skilled and confident performers.
  • Encourage creativity, rhythm, and artistic expression.
  • Provide structured global learning opportunities.
  • Empower future Gurus and Nattuvanars.
About the Guru

A lineage devoted to rhythm, repertoire and the revival of classical form.

Dr. Ranjani Ghoshal

Trained under her father and Guru, Dr. K. Rajamanickam, and now Director of Kalaiaruvi — School of Dance and Music, Dr. Ranjani Ghoshal has spent over two decades shaping performers, teachers and choreographers — rooted in tradition, open to innovation.

Avartana was founded to carry that teaching beyond the classroom: an exclusive platform for Nattuvangam, rhythm studies and Bharatanatyam repertoire, built for serious students anywhere in the world.
